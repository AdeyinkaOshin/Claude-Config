# Calendar QA (Adeyinka) — v1.1, 26 Nov 2026

Bunmi asked (25 Nov 2026): "Can Adeyinka play quality assurance and backup to support Udeme on all
the things I put on the WhatsApp group?" And: "The use of my time and planning diligently and
efficiently is almost like the foundation of all the things we need to do... My time is one of the
most expensive things we have."

Bunmi's goal: **wake up, look at the calendar, and go where it says.** Bunmi measures outcomes, not
activity. Sending a look-ahead is not done. The calendar being right is done.

## Who does what

- **Udeme and the calendar team:** send the scheduling emails, book and move meetings.
- **Adeyinka (with this skill):** checks that their work is complete and correct, follows up with
  Udeme on anything stuck, and steps in as backup when Udeme is out or overloaded.
- **Claude:** reads, checks, drafts. Never books, moves, accepts, declines or sends anything.

## Definition of done

1. Nothing Bunmi asks for in the WhatsApp group gets dropped.
2. Every day has a theme, so Bunmi knows what the day is for.
3. The next 2 weeks are planned: each day's top 3 meetings locked, each week at least 50% planned.
   (Move to 3 weeks once 2 is consistent.)
4. Every external meeting has a purpose, a wanted outcome and context from last time.
5. Every meeting that happened has a next date locked or requested (or is clearly the last one).
6. Weekly score: % of planned meetings that happened as planned goes up.

## Bunmi's standard (the most important part of this skill)

Source: Bunmi's calendar walkthrough with the EA team, Tue 24 Nov 2026 (auto-transcribed, so quotes
are cleaned up from a rough transcript). Every check below comes from here. When a check
and this section disagree, this section wins. Bunmi will come back in 1–2 weeks to see it working.

### 1. Outcomes, not activity
- "You might have sent it… but the week is empty." Sharing a look-ahead is activity. **The week
  actually being on the calendar is the outcome.** Never report a step as done because something was
  sent.
- "Evidence or nothing is happening." Judge by what's on the calendar, not by what's in someone's
  head or a document. "You guys have a lot in your head or in a document somewhere. It's not on the
  calendar." If someone has told a person "you're meeting Bunmi Friday", it must be on the calendar.
- "I'm not expecting perfection… I'm just expecting that we're making progress."

### 2. Plan forward, not day-before
- Scheduling Wednesday on Tuesday means a weak hit rate, because "people have already planned their
  own week". **Plan 2 weeks ahead** (3 weeks once 2 is consistent; 4 is too ambitious).
- Not full days: **"my days can have 3 of the most important things already sorted, that everything
  else will organise around."** A week is done when it's at least 50% planned.
- "By Monday… you should already know what Friday should look like."
- Locked means locked. Once a meeting is on the calendar, it holds. Something new that comes in fits
  around it. If something must move, the team emails properly. Before moving it, ask: can someone
  represent Bunmi? Could Bunmi just drop in for 5 minutes?

### 3. Walk the week the way Bunmi does
When presenting a week, follow Bunmi's order:
1. **What is the week about?** (the focus, e.g. banks, the retreat, new execs)
2. **What are the key days?**
3. **What is each day's anchor?** (e.g. "Monday's anchor: bank / investment and governors")
4. Then the time blocks.

### 4. Every meeting has a reason and a wanted outcome
- "What do we want from this person? Are we going to get it in that meeting?" That's why the agenda
  goes out ahead of time.
- The other side should never hear only "Bunmi wants to meet you tomorrow". They should know why,
  with context from **the last 3 meetings** (e.g. "we're now getting to the MoU", "we're scoping the
  pilot").
- Key meetings get a **one-page brief**: our outcome, the 3–4 things we want, who the right expert
  in VGG is.
- **Bunmi shouldn't be in meetings that others own.** If a meeting is only there because nobody else
  took ownership, flag it as "can be delegated" with a suggested owner.

### 5. Lock the next meeting the same day
For every meeting that happened today, before the day ends:
- Read the notes (the meeting review folder's `03_followups/next-meeting.md` and `02_actions.md`;
  Fireflies directly once shared). If a next date was agreed, it goes on the calendar now.
- If no date was agreed but they clearly need to meet again, the scheduling team emails the same day:
  "Thank you so much for your time on the call today. Ahead of the meeting notes, we'd like to lock in
  a date for your next meeting. Could you share your availability?"
- If the conversation shows it was the end, close it.
- No recording: ask someone who was in the room (Adeyinka is the context person).
- The goal: "maybe 50% of our meetings, the next meeting is already scheduled". In 3–4 months, half
  of each week books itself from the last meetings, with a pre-agenda and who needs to do what.
- For the most important meetings of the day, a **WhatsApp draft** Bunmi can forward: "Thanks, we
  agreed these 4 things."

### 6. Contacts are not equal, and the pattern changes by week
- Know who each person is: capital source, paying customer, investor, direct report, internal staff.
  "Somebody who will give me money" and "somebody who is just reporting back to me" are not equal.
- **Standing pattern:** banks and investment companies almost every day; the new execs (Modupe,
  Lumi, Nosi and the others) almost every other day.
- **This week's priority can shift the pattern** (e.g. the retreat is coming, so the new execs come
  first).
- New execs work from the office (target 80% office use). Bunmi can then do 10–15 minute check-ins in
  person instead of hour-long virtual ones.

### 7. Theme days, built on the connector model
VGG's job is to be the connector between: **startups** (including VGG's own companies),
**capital providers** ("the oxygen"), **distribution / corporates** (MTN, Flour Mills, oil and gas)
and **platforms**. Bunmi should spend about 80% of time here. Theme days group these by sector so
synergy happens: a real estate day, a tech day, a bank/capital day, a customer day. Invite the
relevant startups in ("we are family, when are you coming here?") on their sector's day.

### 8. Work backwards from what matters
If Monday has a big external meeting, the internal prep meeting goes 2–3 working days before
(Wednesday or Thursday). When the week's focus is banks and two bank meetings are already on Monday
and Tuesday, slot the other bank calls around those days.

Planning starts at the end, not at Monday morning. For every **fixed point** coming up (a big
external meeting, a trip, the retreat, a deadline, a deal milestone like an MoU signing or pilot
start), chain back everything that must happen first, and give each step a date:

```
Fixed point (date)
  ← brief to Bunmi            evening before
  ← agenda / one-pager sent   2 working days before (48h rule for Tier B/C)
  ← internal prep meeting     2–3 working days before
  ← documents ready           before the prep meeting (from 03_menu.md / Bunmi's promises)
  ← people briefed            who needs context first (e.g. "brief Fisayo, Uloma, Lumi")
  ← invite sent / slot locked BOOK-BY date (see lead times)
```

**Lead times (defaults; Adeyinka can change them):**

| Meeting | Invite sent / slot locked at least |
|---|---|
| External: capital, customer, partner, government | 10 working days before (2 weeks) |
| External, follow-up already agreed in the last meeting | 5 working days before |
| New execs, direct reports, internal | 2 working days before |
| Office check-ins (15 min, in person) | same week is fine |

A step whose date has already passed is **late**: flag it and propose the fastest fix (shorter
prep, a stand-in, or moving the fixed point if it's flexible). Most "random" weeks come from
booking after the book-by date, so the Friday score checks this.

### 9. How the calendar itself is built
- Bunmi depends on the calendar for everything. If it's cluttered, Bunmi "can't think straight".
- **Lengths on Bunmi's calendar:** internal 15 min, internal strategy 30 min, customer 45 min. The
  meeting can run longer on everyone else's calendar. Bunmi's calendar shows only the slot Bunmi
  joins. When the other side sends the invite, ask them for Bunmi's part of it.
- **1 hour of deep work** every day, plus **flexible blocks** for spontaneous things.
- **Visibility-only items** go on as "free" with a colour category, so they don't block time.
- **Light days** (internal check-ins only) can take more 15-minute blocks.

### 10. Understand, don't just collect
- "She has a lot of the information, but she does not understand it. If you understand it, you will
  know that something is wrong."
- When a meeting on the calendar doesn't make sense, ask: **why is this here? Why is that missing?**
- Adeyinka is "the quality checker and the quality giver": others rely on this information.
  Challenge Claude's output and the team's output.
- The fastest way to understand: **attend the meetings**, in person or virtually.
- Later, not yet: the 5 deal stages (NDA → MoU → pilot scope → project plan → capital to scale) and
  who in VGG owns each relationship. "Don't overwhelm yourself with this one yet."

### 11. Measure every week
- What % of what was planned actually happened as planned?
- For each miss, why? Bunmi was spontaneous · something came up · booked the day before so people
  weren't free · people didn't know why they were coming.
- 10–20% missed is fine. 80% random means something is broken: find out why.

### Order of priority (Bunmi's own list)
1. **Context:** the same sources Bunmi's AI uses (Fireflies first).
2. **Calendar:** lock next meetings daily; plan 2 weeks ahead with themes and anchors.
3. **Understanding:** know why each meeting exists, so you can spot what's wrong.

### Clashes with Favour's plan

Favour's weekly draft and this skill's look-ahead plan the same calendar. Whenever both exist
for the same days, compare them and list every **clash**:
- the same meeting at different days, times or lengths;
- a meeting locked or "to book" in one plan and missing or dropped in the other;
- a different day theme, anchor or top-3 for the same day;
- a different book-by date or owner;
- a slot one plan gives to one meeting and the other plan to another.

For each clash: what Favour's draft says, what the look-ahead says, which source supports which
(Bunmi's instruction, the calendar as it stands, the WhatsApp group, the Calendar Brief), and a
recommendation. **Never pick a winner silently and never change Favour's draft.** Adeyinka settles
it with Favour; only what they can't settle goes to Bunmi as a decision. Small wording differences
are not clashes. If Favour's draft can't be found, say once: "Not compared: Favour's draft not
available", and carry on.

## Mode 0: Plan (Thursday, refreshed Friday) — build the 2-week look-ahead and the team context file

This is where the week is built. Everything follows Bunmi's standard above. Claude drafts;
Adeyinka checks it, then shares the context file with Udeme and the calendar team, who book.

**A. Gather**
1. **Bunmi's calendar** for the next 2 weeks (Outlook, as shared to Adeyinka): what's already locked.
2. **Open requests:** Station flights in the "Bunmi calendar" workstream, plus anything in the BA
   Calendar WhatsApp group not yet on the Station.
3. **Backlog:** the P1/P2/P3 file (once created), or the latest backlog list in the WhatsApp group.
4. **Next meetings owed:** meeting review folders from the last 2 weeks. For each, read
   `03_followups/next-meeting.md` and check the calendar: locked, requested, or owed (standard §5).
   Use `01_brief.md` / `06_next-prebrief.md` for the reason and agenda line of each "to book" item.
5. **Weekly context:** if the `bunmi-weekly-context-file` skill is available, run it (or read its
   latest output) for deals, owners, tiers and where Bunmi will be. Once Logan's sources are live, also
   read Bunmi's Fireflies and vault (see Context sources). Say which sources were missing.
6. **Week focus:** ask Adeyinka in one line if it isn't clear from the above ("Is next week about
   banks, the retreat, new execs, something else?").

**B. Build each week, in Bunmi's order (standard §3)**
1. **What the week is about:** one line.
2. **Key days:** which days matter most and why.
3. **Day theme and anchor** for every day: the theme (capital/bank, customer, startup/portfolio,
   real estate, tech, new execs and internal) and the one anchor meeting the day is built around.
4. **Top 3 per day:** the three most important meetings, already locked or to lock first. Priority:
   capital and paying customers → new execs → direct reports and internal (standard §6).
5. **Work backwards (standard §8)** — do this before filling any day:
   a. List every fixed point in the next **6 weeks** (not just 2): big external meetings, trips,
      the retreat, deadlines, deal milestones (from `02_actions.md` due dates, `03_menu.md`
      promised documents, Station flights with due dates, Bunmi's replies in the EA mailbox).
   b. For each, build its backwards chain with dates (brief → agenda → prep → documents →
      people briefed → book-by).
   c. Every chain step that falls inside the next 2 weeks goes onto the plan as a slot or a task
      with an owner. Steps due today or already past are **late** and go to the top of the gap list.
   d. Book-by dates for meetings 2–6 weeks out go into the team context file, so invites go out
      on time instead of the week before.
6. **Standing pattern (standard §6):** banks / investment almost daily, new execs every other day,
   unless the week's focus changes it. Show where each lands.
7. **Blocks (standard §9):** 1 hour deep work and a flexible block each day; lengths 15 / 30 / 45;
   visibility-only items as "free".
8. **Fill:** place backlog items and owed next-meetings into the matching theme days. Stop at a
   sensible load: aim for at least 50% planned, not a full day.
9. **Gaps:** any day without an anchor or top 3; any P1 with no slot; any big meeting with no prep.

**C. Write two files** (in Adeyinka's OneDrive `Planning/` folder once shared; until then, show
them in chat for her to save or paste)

`Lookahead-<YYYY-MM-DD>.md` — the 2-week look-ahead (for Bunmi and the team):
```
Next 2 weeks | <dates> | <cities>
The 2 weeks in 3 lines (for Bunmi)

Week 1: <what it's about>
  Key days: ...
  <Day date> | Theme: <theme> | Anchor: <meeting>
     Top 3:  1. <meeting> (locked / to book)  2. ...  3. ...
     Blocks: deep work <time> · flex <time>
     Prep:   <internal prep for which big meeting>
  ...
Week 2: (same)

Working backwards (next 6 weeks)
  <Fixed point> (<date>)
     book-by <date> · prep <date> · documents <date> · agenda <date> · brief <date>
     status: on track / LATE (<which step>)

Gaps to close   numbered, each with what to do (late chain steps first)
```

`Context-for-calendar-team-<YYYY-MM-DD>.md` — what Udeme's team needs to book (one page per week):
```
Week focus and themes   one line + the theme for each day
Locked (don't move)     meeting · day/time · why it matters
To book, in this order  BOOK-BY date · who · why / wanted outcome · theme day to aim for ·
                        length (15/30/45) · Bunmi's slot only · agenda line for the invite · owner
                        (sorted by book-by date: earliest first; late ones marked LATE)
Coming up (3–6 weeks)   fixed points further out and the date their invite must go
Next meetings owed      from meetings already held: who · proposed window · email to send (§5 wording)
Prep meetings needed    internal prep · before which meeting · by when
Rules for this week     anything Bunmi said (e.g. "Kase Lawal: Bunmi handles personally",
                        "Ayo Allu: in person only, when back")
Open questions          only things Adeyinka or Bunmi must decide
```
Never include family or personal details (show "held"). Every "to book" line must have a reason and
a wanted outcome (standard §4); if one is unknown, put it under Open questions instead of guessing.

**D. Compare with Favour's plan** — run "Clashes with Favour's plan" above for the 2 weeks.

**E. Hand over**

2. Offer a short WhatsApp/Teams note to Udeme to go with the context file. Adeyinka sends it.

- **Understanding check (standard §4, §10):** for each meeting, can you say why it's there and what
  Bunmi wants from it? If not, flag "why is this here?". If it's a meeting someone else should own,

  meetings, flag it.

  stand-in or a 5-minute drop-in considered first?
