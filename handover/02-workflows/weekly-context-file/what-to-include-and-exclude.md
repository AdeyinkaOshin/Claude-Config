# Context file: what to include and exclude

## Include

| Item | Where it comes from |
|---|---|
| Where Bunmi is each day (look-ahead) | Flights, hotels, trip entries on Bunmi's calendar |
| Gaps between flights (e.g. a departure from a city with no flight to get there) | Bunmi's calendar |
| Calendar clashes, including meetings during flights or events | Bunmi's calendar |
| In-person meetings set in a city Bunmi isn't in | Calendar cross-checked with location |
| Priority meetings, tier 1–3, with VGG owner, status and context | BA - Calendar & Technical Team sync chat (tiers); meeting folders (owner, context); calendar (booked or not) |
| Other live items that need Bunmi | Meeting folders (`01_brief.md`, `02_actions.md`, `status.md`); Bunmi <> EA Team chat |
| Dates to protect (boards, retreats, deadlines) | Calendar, chats, folders |
| Last week at a glance (for Bunmi's weekly report) | Calendar and folders for the covered week |
| What changed this week (NEW / UP / DOWN / REMOVED / BOOKED / DATE MOVED / DON'T BOOK / TRAVEL) | Compared with last week's file |
| Open questions, most urgent first | Anything the sources don't settle |

## Tiers

- **1** = must happen this visit or week
- **2** = important, book after tier 1
- **3** = book if there's room; the owner can lead
- Tier sits on the **meeting or deal**, not the person.
- Tiers come **only** from the sync list or Bunmi. The meeting folders use A/B/C as a review
  level. **Never turn A/B/C into 1/2/3.**
- Tier 1–3 meetings need a one-page pre-read from the owner 24 hours ahead. If missing, the
  meeting is cut to 15 minutes or moved, and the owner is told.

## Exclude (keep-out list)

- Personal and family items: private appointments, school runs, family office, birthdays,
  personal finance.
- Health matters.
- Relationship sensitivities.
- Phone numbers and logistics contacts.
- Tier 4–5 meetings.
- Personal WhatsApp chats (skip them entirely).
- Raw transcript text, unless the brief is missing (and then only the facts needed).
- Anything guessed. Missing facts are "TBC" or "not stated".

## Flags, not fixes

Claude flags these and leaves them for a person to settle:

- duplicates (same counterpart at two tiers);
- name spellings that differ between sources;
- items listed under the wrong city;
- owners still missing;
- overdue commitments.
