# Friday check rules (A1 to H1), in plain English

This is a summary for a new person. The master rules are in the `friday-calendar-check`
skill. The current **Calendar Brief** supplies the tiers, heads-down times, day themes,
to-book list and seasonal rules. Every finding cites one of these rule IDs.

## Order of authority

1. Bunmi's direct instruction (newest first)
2. The current Calendar Brief
3. The working bibles CT-00 to CT-04
4. Past practice

## Time zones

The check never assumes an offset. It works it out from each meeting's date, using a table of
offsets for the travel period (including clock changes abroad). Every line shows Lagos time
and the time where Bunmi is.

## A. Shape of the plan

| Rule | Check |
|---|---|
| A1 | The draft starts from the brief's to-book list. Every item appears, or the draft says why not. |
| A2 | Four-week view: this week in detail, next three in outline. Known trips and planning tracks appear. |
| A3 | Sections in order: Anchors · Last week · This week day by day · For your visibility · Decisions needed · Backlog · Next three weeks · Ahead. |
| A4 | "Last week" says what slipped and why. Nothing buried. |
| A5 | Every line shows both time zones, a status (Confirmed · Proposed · Rescheduling) and, for external meetings, a named owner. |

## B. Protected time

| Rule | Check |
|---|---|
| B1 | Heads-down blocks at the brief's times, at least two hours every weekday, nothing booked into them. |
| B2 | Personal blocks untouched and shown only as "held". Never described. |
| B3 | Two afternoons a week protected as execution time. Named. Fewer than two is a finding. |
| B4 | Anything touching protected time goes under "Decisions needed", never shown as booked. |

## C. Mornings and geography (while Bunmi is abroad)

| Rule | Check |
|---|---|
| C1 | Nigeria calls start no earlier than the brief's earliest time in Bunmi's local morning. |
| C2 | The early block of intense calls runs on two days a week only. |
| C3 | Calls with people elsewhere are in *their* working hours (flag before 08:00 or after 18:00 their time). |
| C4 | Afternoons in Bunmi's location are for local in-person meetings only. No Lagos calls. |
| C5 | Local in-person meetings sit on the set days, grouped, with travel time each way. |

Exact times and days are in the skill and the Calendar Brief.

## D. Spacing and clashes

| Rule | Check |
|---|---|
| D1 | No double bookings (end time worked out from length). |
| D2 | No more than two calls back to back; 15 minutes free after every second call. |
| D3 | Every clash is under "Decisions needed": options, cost of each, a recommendation. Never accept both. |
| D4 | Lengths follow CT-02 (60 / 45 / 30 / 15 by meeting type). See the conflict note in the README, section 9. |

## E. Priorities and tiers

| Rule | Check |
|---|---|
| E1 | Higher tier keeps the slot. Fixed outside dates go first. |
| E2 | External meetings booked 2–6 weeks out, clustered by theme and place. Flag short-notice ones that aren't tier 1–2, fixed, or on the to-book list. |
| E3 | Seasonal rule during the trip: tier 5 waits until after the trip or goes to a delegate. |
| E4 | Meetings Bunmi need not attend go in "For your visibility", with a reason. |
| E5 | While travelling, standing meetings are 45 minutes in Lagos hours, chaired by their owner, and show whether and how long Bunmi joins. |

## F. Pre-reads by tier

| Rule | Check |
|---|---|
| F1 | Tier 1–3: one-page pre-read from the owner 24 hours ahead (board packs 48). Due time shown. |
| F2 | Tier 4–5: one line of context from Favour is enough. |
| F3 | Tier 1–3 with no pre-read 24h ahead: cut to 15 minutes or move; owner told why. No escalation to Bunmi. |

## G. Touchpoints with Bunmi

| Rule | Check |
|---|---|
| G1 | Only three regular touchpoints: morning plan with 30-minute reminders, Sunday final, decisions. No other recurring messages. |
| G2 | Contact issues that need Bunmi go into the Sunday final as exceptions. |

## H. Learning

| Rule | Check |
|---|---|
| H1 | Anything also in last week's note is marked **REPEAT** and should become a rule in the relevant CT document. |

## Not flagged

- Times fixed by the brief's to-book list (skip the tier check; still checked for clashes,
  spacing and time zones).
- Meetings Bunmi scheduled personally (clashes only).
- Personal block contents. Never.
