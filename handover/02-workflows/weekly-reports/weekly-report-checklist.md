# Weekly report — check before you submit

Use this on every draft of `Logan_Weekly_Report_<MonDD>-<DD>.docx`. Tick each line.
If one fails, tell Claude in the same chat and let it regenerate the file.

## Header
- [ ] Week shown is the right Monday – Friday, spelled out (e.g. "23 – 27 November 2026").
- [ ] Prepared by = the person actually preparing it. Submitted to = Central Ops, Omotola.
- [ ] File name follows `Logan_Weekly_Report_<MonDD>-<DD>.docx`.

## The six sections, in this order
- [ ] **Priorities for the Week** — bullets from the kickoff/planning messages plus mid-week additions.
- [ ] **Closed Out This Week** — one sub-heading per item or meeting. Meetings with substance have a
      date/time/attendees line and one bullet per decision, with a bold lead-in.
- [ ] **Pending / Carried Into Next Week** — a 3-column table: Item | Status | Next Step. Every open loop is here.
- [ ] **Escalations / Collisions** — either real items, or the line "No items met the 5-working-day
      blocker threshold this week." Scheduling churn and technical failures noted.
- [ ] **Context Surfaced for Logan's Decisions This Week** — what the team gave Logan that shaped a call.
- [ ] **Priorities Going Into Next Week** — one line per open thread.

## Content
- [ ] Specific: names, times, numbers, exact asks. No "feedback was given" without saying what.
- [ ] Nothing invented. Gaps are labelled as gaps.
- [ ] Anything you know has since happened is moved from Pending to Closed Out.
- [ ] Anything you were told to leave out is gone completely.
- [ ] Only Logan's workstream (not Banke's reporting).
- [ ] No phone numbers, family/health/personal details, compensation or financial terms.

## Layout
- [ ] Header and footer show on every page.
- [ ] Tables fit inside the margins; no cut-off text.
- [ ] Claude says it rendered and looked at the pages after the last edit.
