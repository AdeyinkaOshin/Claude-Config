# Day themes, anchors and the standing pattern

## Why themes

VGG's job is to connect four groups: **startups** (including VGG's own companies),
**capital providers**, **distribution / corporates** and **platforms**. Bunmi should spend
about 80% of their time on this. Theme days group meetings by sector so connections happen
on the same day, and the right startups can be invited in on their sector's day.

Every day gets:

- a **theme**: what the day is for;
- an **anchor**: the one meeting the day is built around;
- a **top 3**: the three most important meetings, locked first. Everything else fits around them.

## Theme menu (from the skill)

| Theme | Who goes there |
|---|---|
| Capital / bank day | Banks, investors, capital providers |
| Customer day | Large corporates and deals to close |
| Startup / portfolio day | Startups and VGG's own companies, linked to the platforms |
| Real estate day | Real estate partners and projects |
| Tech day | Technology partners and platforms |
| New execs and internal | New executives (in the office, every other day) and direct reports |

## Standing pattern

- Banks and investment companies: **almost every day**.
- New executives: **almost every other day**, ideally short in-person check-ins at the office.
- The week's focus can change this (for example, a retreat week puts the new execs first).

## Priority when slotting

1. Capital and paying customers
2. New execs
3. Direct reports and internal

## Blocks every day

- 1 hour of deep work.
- A flexible block for spontaneous things.
- Information-only items set as "free" with a colour category, so they don't block time.
- Lengths on Bunmi's calendar: internal 15 min, internal strategy 30 min, customer 45 min.
  Bunmi's calendar shows only the slot Bunmi joins.
- Light days (internal check-ins only) can take more 15-minute blocks.

## Presenting a week (Bunmi's order)

1. What is the week about?
2. What are the key days?
3. What is each day's anchor?
4. Then the time blocks.

*Example (invented):* "Week of 7 Dec is about capital. Key days: Tue and Thu. Mon anchor:
new-exec planning session. Tue anchor: bank partner review. ..."
