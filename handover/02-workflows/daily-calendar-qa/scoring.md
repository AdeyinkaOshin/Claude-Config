# Weekly score (Friday score mode)

Bunmi wants the calendar measured every week. The score is simple:

> **What % of the meetings planned for the week actually happened as planned?**

## How Claude scores it

1. Take the plan for the week as it stood after the **Sunday check**.
2. Compare it with what happened (Bunmi's Outlook calendar and the meeting review folders).
3. Score = meetings that happened as planned ÷ meetings planned.
4. For each miss, give one reason:
   - Bunmi moved it (spontaneous change)
   - booked too late (people weren't free)
   - purpose unclear (people didn't know why they were coming)
   - other side unavailable
   - other (say what)
5. For "booked too late": was it booked after its **book-by date**? Report how many meetings
   this week were booked **on time vs late**. See
   [`../two-week-look-ahead/working-backwards-and-lead-times.md`](../two-week-look-ahead/working-backwards-and-lead-times.md).
6. Pick **one** fix to try next week.
7. Draft a short note to Udeme with the score and the fix. Adeyinka sends it.

## How to read it

| Missed | Meaning |
|---|---|
| 10–20% | Fine. Some change is normal. |
| Much more | Something is broken. Find out why from the miss reasons. |
| Around 80% random | The planning isn't working. Look at book-by dates and purposes first. |

Bunmi isn't expecting perfection, only progress. The goal is for the score to go up week on
week.

*Example (invented):* 20 meetings planned, 15 happened as planned → 75%. Misses: 2 booked
too late, 2 moved by Bunmi, 1 other side unavailable. On time vs late: 14 / 6. Fix to try:
send all capital-meeting invites by their book-by date.
