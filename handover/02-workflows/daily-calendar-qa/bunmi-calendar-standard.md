# Bunmi's calendar standard (summary)

Source: Bunmi's calendar walkthrough with the EA team, Tue 24 Nov 2026. The full version is
the "Bunmi's standard" section of the `calendar-qa` skill. **When a check and the standard
disagree, the standard wins.** Bunmi planned to come back in 1–2 weeks to see it working.

This page is a plain-English summary for a new person. It does not replace the skill.

1. **Outcomes, not activity.** Sent is not done. On the calendar is done. If someone has been
   told "you're meeting Bunmi Friday", it must be on the calendar. Bunmi expects progress,
   not perfection.
2. **Plan forward.** Plan 2 weeks ahead (3 once 2 is consistent). Each day needs its 3 most
   important meetings sorted; everything else fits around them. A week is done when at least
   50% planned. By Monday, the team should know what Friday looks like. **Locked means
   locked**: new things fit around it. Before moving one, ask if someone can represent Bunmi,
   or if Bunmi can drop in for 5 minutes.
3. **Walk the week Bunmi's way.** What is the week about → key days → each day's anchor →
   time blocks.
4. **Every meeting has a reason and a wanted outcome.** The other side knows why they are
   coming, with context from the last 3 meetings. Key meetings get a one-page brief. Bunmi
   shouldn't be in meetings others should own: flag "can be delegated".
5. **Lock the next meeting the same day.** After each meeting, the next date goes on the
   calendar, or the team emails that day to ask for availability, or the thread is closed.
   Goal: about half of each week books itself from earlier meetings.
6. **Contacts are not equal.** Capital sources and paying customers come before people
   reporting back. Standing pattern: banks/investment almost daily; new execs every other day.
   The week's priority can change the pattern.
7. **Theme days** built on VGG's connector role. See [`day-themes.md`](day-themes.md).
8. **Work backwards** from every fixed point. See
   [`../two-week-look-ahead/working-backwards-and-lead-times.md`](../two-week-look-ahead/working-backwards-and-lead-times.md).
9. **A clean calendar.** Lengths on Bunmi's calendar: internal 15, internal strategy 30,
   customer 45 minutes (Bunmi's slot only). 1 hour deep work and a flex block daily.
   Information-only items set to "free" with a colour.
10. **Understand, don't just collect.** Ask "why is this here? why is that missing?" Adeyinka
    is the quality checker and the quality giver. The fastest way to understand is to attend
    the meetings.
11. **Measure every week.** See [`scoring.md`](scoring.md).

**Bunmi's own priority order:** context first (the same sources Bunmi's own Claude uses),
then the calendar, then understanding why each meeting exists.
